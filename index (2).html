Frequently Asked

Dabba Lite is a WiFi hotspot device that, once deployed by your Local Cable Operator, earns you daily $DBT token rewards. It connects to India's decentralized internet infrastructure and broadcasts public WiFi to nearby users.

No. The Dabba Lite is deployed by a Local Cable Operator (LCO) in your area. You purchase the device; the LCO handles installation, connectivity, and maintenance. Your role is simply to own it and earn from it.

Hotspot owners receive 60% of all network rewards. In Year 1, rewards are 100% UBI from a 700 million $DBT community pool, distributed daily. Actual earnings depend on $DBT price and network parameters, with 10% annual decay applied each year.

$DBT is Dabba Network's native utility token on the Solana blockchain, used to pay rewards to hotspot owners, LCOs, and other network participants. It can be traded on decentralized exchanges like Jupiter.

Dabba Network (originally WiFi Dabba) was founded in 2017 and is backed by Y Combinator, Multicoin Capital, and Borderless Capital. With 13,000+ devices sold and 5,000+ deployed across India, it is one of the largest active DePIN networks in Asia.

There is a one-time activation fee of $100 equivalent in $DBT, split across four components: device attachment ($10), LCO lease ($70), commissioning ($10), and marketing ($10). This activates your hotspot on the network and enables reward generation.

The full tokenomics documentation is available at gitbook.dabba.network. It covers supply schedules, reward distribution mechanics, pool allocations, and governance parameters in detail.
